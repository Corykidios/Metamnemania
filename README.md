# Μεταμνημανία [Metamnemania]
## Epistemological Exegesis
**με + μετά + ἀμνή + μνῆμα + νῆμα + ἧμα + μανία + ἀνία**
. με [me]: accusative singular of ἐγώ.
. ἐγώ [e.gō]: I. I at least, for my part, indeed, for myself. myself, the self, the ego.
. μετά [me.ta]: in the midst of, among, between. in common, along with, by aid of, in cooperation with, by aid of, with, together with, companions, in conjunction with. in one's dealings, by means of. between, among others; among, in company with, belonging to, between two parties; of parts of persons, between, to hold between in the hands, of a child being born, to fall between her feet. to complete a number, besides, over and above. f motion, into the middle of, coming into or among, especially where a number of persons is implied, into the midst; in pursuit or quest of, of persons, in a friendly sense, to seek go after, in search of, seek after. of sequence or succession; of place, after, behind, beyond, on the far side; of time, after, next to, thereupon, there-after; of worth, rank, next after, next to. after, according to, as you and I wish. generally, among, between, including. among them, with them; and then, next afterwards; thereafter, when past. of community or participation; of action in common with another; in the midst of, of space or time, between; of succession of time; of pursuit; of letting go; after, behind; reversely; most frequently of change of place, condition, plan.
. ἀμνή [am.nē]: ewe-lamb.
. μνῆμα [mnē.ma]: memorial, remembrance, record of a person or thing. mound or building in honour of the dead, monument, tomb, coffin. memorial dedicated toa god, monument. memory.
. νῆμα [nē.ma]: to spin. that which is spun, thread, yarn. thread of a spider's web, of the Fates. destiny. silk sutures.
. ἧμα [ē.ma]: that which is thrown, dart, javelin, darting.
. μανία [ma.ni.a]: madness; enthusiasm, inspired frenzy; passion, mad desire. μανότης.
. μανότης [ma.no.tēs]: looseness of texture, porousness. rarity, separateness.
. ἀνία [a.ni.a]: grief, sorrow, distress, trouble; a mischief. the killjoy, bane, an annoyance.
