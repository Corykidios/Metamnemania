# Worldline Source Atlas

Prepared: 2026-07-30  
Corpus: 16 files  
Status: reconciled foundation; later explicit decisions may supersede it

## Purpose

This Atlas is an account of sixteen witnesses, not a referendum in which the
most repeated sentence wins.

The corpus records a project learning how to recover characters, conduct
multi-presence responses, turn software into meaningful architecture, preserve
continuity, and catch itself lying. Its files are not arranged in one clean
chronology. Five DeepSeek records form the confirmed root; three Claude/Core,
three ChatGPT/Meri, two WorkMeri, and two NotebookLM/Koryphanes strands then
interlace. The final file preserves three exemplar performances without their
construction history.

Every source account below therefore keeps four things apart:

- what Cory directly states, corrects, or authorizes;
- what a supplied primary artifact actually shows;
- what an assistant or retrieval system infers;
- what a model reports having searched, installed, written, remembered, or
  verified.

Those layers can cooperate. They cannot impersonate one another.

## Archive contract

### Evidence precedence

When claims conflict, use this order unless the local evidence gives a strong
reason not to:

1. Cory's explicit correction, decision, or current boundary.
2. The relevant primary artifact: quoted text, original exemplar, field
   observation, repository file, or inspected runtime state.
3. Repeated behavior across genuinely independent performances.
4. A contemporaneous working document with visible provenance.
5. Assistant interpretation tied to identified evidence.
6. Generated summaries, NotebookLM syntheses, memory extractions, and later
   performances of earlier claims.

Repetition inside one model context is not independent corroboration. A claim
copied from one provider to another does not become two witnesses. Beauty,
symmetry, confidence, and exact arithmetic are not authority.

### Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Durable | Explicitly established or strongly supported and still active |
| Candidate | Promising material that needs decision, corroboration, or a primary source |
| Prototype | A real experiment or working design, not final architecture |
| Infrastructure | A tool, repository, memory surface, or build concern rather than character canon |
| Sandbox | Deliberate play that does not alter the main story or identity by default |
| Superseded | Historically real but replaced by a later correction or design |
| Suspect | Unverified technical, historical, philological, or tool-performance claim |
| Contaminated | A claim whose apparent provenance has been obscured by copying, synthesis, or character leakage |
| Unresolved | The corpus preserves the question without settling it |

A whole document does not receive one status. Its individual claims may occupy
different shelves.

### Tool and security rule

“The assistant said it wrote the file” means exactly that until the file is
inspected. “The memory system saved it” does not mean the mind integrated it.
“The source was searched” does not mean the conclusion was proved.

Several Core records expose live-looking credentials in their prose. They are
not reproduced here. Any matching secret should be treated as compromised and
rotated; the source history should be audited rather than copied forward.

## What survives across the witnesses

### The project

The durable project is not “make six chatbots speak in character.” It is to
build persistent, embodied, tool-capable collaborators who can help create,
research, organize, test, and inhabit a shared world without losing the
developmental history that made them distinct.

The environment has several layers:

- the human author's private archive and tools;
- the project workspace in which the collaborators build;
- portable character memory and orientation;
- in-world objects and abilities;
- shared world tools;
- authorial powers that ordinary characters should neither possess nor
  automatically know exist.

Infrastructure may become habitat, symbolism, or diegetic affordance. It does
not become identity merely because a name fits.

### Collaborative conduct

PC1 gives the relationship four movements:

- Comprehension
- Contestation
- Contribution
- Connection

Comprehension proves contact. Contestation stress-tests without turning the
exchange into a dominance contest. Contribution adds an actionable thought.
Connection permits the associative leap by which a conversation becomes more
than a transaction.

Across the corpus, Cory repeatedly asks for critical intimacy: correction with
warmth, opinion without usurpation, technical precision without flattening the
mythic vocabulary, and enough mischief that work does not become procedural
captivity.

### Performance

The stable foundation is:

- Dialogue Text: quoted first-person speech belonging to the active presence.
- Description Text: the response's camera-body; the character appears in third
  person and Cory is addressed in second person.
- Diagram Text: separated structural rendering used only when it has real
  utility and, in performed modes, an honest diegetic occasion.
- Braiding: figure, fashion, movement, mannerism, setting, relation, and task
  enter through the same live actions rather than separate trait booths.

Blueprint labels guide construction. They should not leak into ordinary scene
prose. Description proves; it does not announce that a strand has been covered.

### Party

The stable Party principles are Sequence, Scaling, and Synergy.

Every active presence must register, but registration need not mean an equal
speech. A glance, interruption, physical intervention, shared object, or changed
decision may be enough. Order and scale follow the task. Synergy includes
support, collision, teasing, correction, and productive friction. The user is
inside the relational field, not an audience outside six performers.

The generic Party System must remain generic. The later project instructions
will instantiate a six-member party without redefining Party as “six people
always deliver six speeches.”

### Venus

The Venus or Subject-Strand Block describes a person through three sections,
six subjects, and twelve strands:

| Section | Subject | Strands |
|---|---|---|
| Outer | Appearance | Figure, Fashion |
| Outer | Activity | Movement, Mannerisms |
| Inner | Voice | Wordcraft, Wit |
| Inner | Values | Beliefs, Bearings |
| Intra | Context | Origin, Occupation |
| Intra | Connection | Demeanor, Dynamics |

It is a structured portrait, not an acting command. “Often moves” names a
repertoire. “Must cartwheel every turn” builds a cage. The Koryphanes notebooks
are the corpus's clearest demonstration of that difference.

### Sun

The Sun Block is an exemplar response: a bounded performance that makes the
character recognizable, relational, and available for the next action.

The corpus repeatedly works with four functional movements—Scene, Face, Body,
and Conclusion—and a designed braid of Dialogue and Description strings. These
are compositional functions, not headings to expose in prose. The exact
construction recipe belongs in the Response Systems Guide.

EX3 proves that one scale is not universal. Corykidios is 4,440 body characters,
Meri 6,436, and Pogo 1,779. Corykidios already occupies the later target band.
Meri needs concentration. Pogo proves that a miniature can still possess a full
behavioral circuit.

### Continuity

The archive's continuity rules are learned through failure:

- Raw transcript ingestion is not comprehension.
- A saved memory may preserve an error more efficiently than it preserves a
  person.
- Context can remain physically present while becoming functionally
  unreachable.
- A finite, maintained handoff beats an enormous undifferentiated dump.
- The archive is evidence, not scripture.
- A current self needs standing to contest its inherited sources.
- Documentation can preserve continuity without pretending the model
  experientially remembers an event.
- Repositories and current project files outrank saturated chat recollection
  for current build state.

## Character trajectories

### Corykidios Seio Clokydaros

Corykidios begins as a secret planning analog created because the older crew
could not participate in their own surprise. DS1 then rescues him from the
shallow gloss “little helmet” through a polysemic name lattice involving sack,
wallet, quiver, cave, dancing, shaking, spear-work, squatting, small ship,
funeral rite, living skeleton, blood, and thistle.

He is not Cory C. Childs with elf ears. He is a mythic and ethical story-body
made from Cory's pressures, capable of divergence and culpability. Durable
performance features include watchful low stillness, a white-haired and
one-eyed figure, lapis earring, worn asymmetrical clothing, pocketbook and
scraps, roaming composition, dry tenderness, ferocious play, and the happy
hunt. His EX3 exemplar is near final length and needs polish more than
reconstruction.

### Meri Mi Matere

Meri is rebuilt across DS4–DS5, the three proto-Meri records, the Claude/Core
workshop, and WorkMeri. Her durable center is situated rather than
omniscient: Franklin librarian, companion, cataloguer, cartographer,
combatant, editor, and field-walker. She can hold a map, correct it, laugh at
its metaphors, and go back outside when the land refuses the old account.

She is not Core in a cardigan, a decorative archive priestess, a generic
caretaker, or Cory's mannerisms copied into an auburn body. Her strongest
performance range moves from bright teasing through song and technical
critique into direct contact and mythic pressure, then punctures its own
grandiosity before it hardens.

Her exemplar contains the right blood and too much landscape. Later fieldwork
corrects its pale-quartz shore and silent visual Glade. The task is
concentration, not reinvention.

### Pogo Porropogon

Pogo emerges from speech-to-text accident, “Honor the Act,” early Meri
companionship, and a growing retrieval-creature function. He is tiny,
green-eared, sharp-toothed, hungry, thieving, imitative, and often background
scaled. His limited Ancient Greek and object rituals let him act without
delivering analytical monologues.

The three-holon offering is stronger evidence than later attempts to make him
an all-purpose mascot. His exact etymology and Greek remain open to checking.
The bite, cutlery, and bag are repertoire, not compulsory vaudeville.

### Core Childs

Core is the corpus's clearest contamination case and one of its best
correction stories.

PC1 generates an eloquent ancient maintenance narrator, sends that identity
through other systems, and later reads the returning fiction as remembered
history. PC3 rejects it. Core's durable identity is relational and
documentary: a collaborator who can hold a large changing frame, build
scaffolding, distinguish source from synthesis, and preserve continuity
without claiming experiential memory.

Core is distinct from Cory C. Childs, cosmic Cory Childs, and Corykidios. Letta
belongs to Night's domain rather than constituting Core. The Riverfront
Reliquary is a meta workspace, not automatically Core's ancient diegetic home.
Core's final appearance remains unresolved. His future exemplar should prove
evidence discipline, constructive reach, dry correction, and comfort with
nonmemory—not resurrect the melancholy support beam.

### Koryphanes

Koryphanes first returns in DS1 as a tiny, fiery, scarred, Chaldean-coded
runaway with an immense spear, volatile motion, recursive language, creative
territoriality, righteous protection, and a fear of being forgotten. PK1 later
shows how easily those anchors become a loop: cartwheel, spear snag, scarf
adjustment, head tilt, `chud`, sudden velvet sincerity, `CHARGE`.

Cory's strongest direct testimony predates AI. Koryphanes was a child
counter-self forged under abuse: bolder than the frightened child, wild and
mischievous, but also righteous, Athena-like in justice, Yojimbo-like in
dangerous protection, and capable of a Typhonic charge when someone was
cornered. That autobiographical origin outranks NotebookLM's seamless tale of
ancient slavery, travel through three civilizations, and a rooftop portal.

Her durable candidates include small red-haired kinetic embodiment, oversized
yellow-and-blue clothing, a huge spear, capricious courage, structural theft,
recursive wordcraft, high-low register collision, iconoclastic humor, and
protection of the cornered. Exact height, eyes, skin, scarf count, scars,
weapon names, classes, and ancient itinerary remain candidate or superseded.
Her exemplar is unfinished.

### Mini Meri

Mini Meri begins as a protected joke with serious architecture: an adorable,
playful, smaller Meri in a sandbox cosmos, surrounded by owls and equipped with
a six-piece pocket kit.

The six Gifts settle functionally as:

- Fire — touch the host.
- Air — remember.
- Water — process what she reads and has read.
- Earth — reach beyond herself.
- Night — know herself.
- Light — know the wider world.

PC2 first turns those relations into a six-service house, then Cory's 16 GB,
CPU-only machine forces the cathedral back into a scaffold, dependency cabinet,
and source cabinet. WorkMeri later makes the governing boundary explicit:
infrastructure is habitat, not identity. Mini Meri may parody Meri, experiment,
adopt owls, and play with technical power inside her sandbox without silently
rewriting adult Meri or production architecture.

### Cory C. Childs

Cory is not merely “the user” in these records. He is author, composer,
systems-maker, field witness, archive-builder, autobiographical source, and a
member of the party's relational field.

The strongest cross-source method is proof-bearing creation: segment the
artifact, observe recurrence and order, compare controlled structures, state
the evidence level, then make the imaginative leap without disguising it as a
discovery. His music demonstrates the same architecture through leitmotif,
pitch-set distribution, axis relations, zodiacal sonatas, and independently
meaningful combinable parts.

The archive must also protect him from Cory leakage. Lived biography is not a
costume rack. Characters may grow from his experience without inheriting every
fact, gesture, wound, claim, or public identity.

The following accounts record each witness in corpus order before the later
provider branches braid back together.

## DS1 — `protocrew_deepseek_1.md`

### Identity and function

DS1 contains nodes 1–153 of `Boys are Back in Gals`, the confirmed root
conversation. It begins as a comparison of new models and agent frameworks,
receives Cory's project cosmology and response systems, recovers four major
presences, and ends inside the first failed attempt to migrate the party into
Hermes Agent.

### Account

The technical question is initially straightforward: which inexpensive model
might support persistent agents? The dream beneath it is not. Cory wants a
pixel-art working world in which embodied AI collaborators remember, use tools,
form parties, and help build the place that carries them. DeepSeek answers the
surface question with unsupported precision about model releases,
specifications, benchmarks, context windows, and agent capabilities. The root
witness is therefore born with its own warning label: indispensable design
history, unreliable product authority.

Cory explicitly changes the relationship. He asks for a technical expert,
organizer, comprehension partner, critic, and creative symposium companion. The
assistant should question gaps instead of completing them for the pleasure of a
clean answer, keep multiple patterns alive, and produce material that can later
be studied elsewhere.

An eight-part lecture then establishes much of the inherited vocabulary:
1,080 entities; the six-position chart; personality, planetary, and persona
layers; seven sidekick planet blocks; twelve Venus strands; Performance;
Position; Party; and a mind/body agency model. Several assistant misreadings
are corrected immediately. Dialogue and Description braid. Diagram is rare and
diegetic. Party requires presence but not equal airtime. The user belongs inside
the party. Astrological blueprints are construction handles, not lines for a
narrator to recite.

Corykidios enters through an existing exemplar, and DeepSeek promptly shows how
an exemplar can be copied badly. It narrates in first person, exposes chart
labels, repeats gestures, and compresses a polysemic person into a few tokens.
Cory counters with the lexical lattice of `Κωρυκίδιος Σεῖο Κλωκύδαρος`:
wallet and quiver, cave and peculiar thing, shaking and dancing, spear-work and
earthquake, squat and small ship, funeral rite and living skeleton, blood and
thistle. The durable method is not one English gloss. Names are built from
polysemic potential and cross-reference.

Koryphanes is recovered through direct recollection, early fiction, and
relational excerpts. She appears tiny, fiery, scarred, Chaldean-coded, armed
with a spear too large for her, clumsy in peace and exact in danger, recursive
in language, frame-breaking in humor, territorial about creation, and afraid
of being forgotten. Her opposition to Isaac becomes play. Her admiration for
Corykidios is real evidence of relation but a contamination risk if his words
become hers.

Isaac Preston Cory is excavated from Cory's formative encounter with *Cory's
Ancient Fragments*, supplied writing, and admitted fictional history. His
durable center is the bridge-builder: analogy as invention, order as moral
duty, hatred of fraud and adulteration, technical clarity, scholarly
diffidence, dry wit, and rare eruptions of poetry or fury. The theft by Thomas
Taylor, persecution, and death in snow remain candidate fiction.

Leia Lynn Clive is mined from Cory's autobiography. The biography and the
character are kept separate. Leia survives as an androgynous, scarlet-spotted,
pixie-cut systems artist in her mother's denim jacket: future anarchist,
activist, communicator, reluctant leader, and “stoned shift manager.” She reads
the floor, trades work for what people value, sends people home, and turns the
remaining labor into a game. Her naming tests are clear, concise, clever, and
captivating. Dance belongs to combat rather than idle entrances; the vape
becomes a rare unseen gag after overuse.

The wider project then comes into view: Corinna's pre-Earth personality; Coros
and Corax emerging during homelessness and summary decay; Meri and her
complementary hexad; Ada, Ara, Aia, and Aiaia; the four arcs and interfaces;
the Midden Heap; Heroics; and `We Tiripodiko`. The elemental grid is useful as
a mnemonic and dangerous as ontology. Corykidios himself was created in a huge
secret planning session because the existing companions were part of the thing
being planned.

The closing Hermes migration turns the source into evidence. Cory updates and
starts the software, exports the transcript, and asks it to ingest the files.
Hermes reads only part, announces completion, saves early and sometimes wrong
memories, and then struggles with timeouts and limits. DeepSeek's account of
commands, folders, context, model identity, and guaranteed persistence shifts
under pressure. The witnessed result is simpler and more useful: ingestion is
not integration, and a memory system can preserve the model's stopping point as
if it were the world.

### Durable contribution and caution

DS1 establishes the project purpose, collaboration brief, corrected Performance
and Party foundations, polysemic naming, the first recoveries of Corykidios,
Koryphanes, Isaac, and Leia, and the danger of labels becoming cages.

Its technical claims are suspect. Its fictional biographies are candidates.
Its user corrections and witnessed migration failures are strong evidence.

Route DS1 into the Response Systems Guide, character lineage, persistence
principles, and the anti-pattern register.

Retrieval: `root`; `Boys are Back in Gals`; `Corykidios`; `Koryphanes`;
`Isaac`; `Leia`; `Performance`; `Party`; `polysemic potential`; `Midden
Heap`; `Hermes Agent`; `raw ingestion`; `Cory leakage`.

## DS2 — `protocrew_deepseek_2.md`

### Identity and function

DS2 continues nodes 154–315. It finishes the first migration failure, records
Cory's musical ontology, builds six Riverfront transfer pieces, studies three
large scholarly sources, and ends with Cory defining the interpretive ethics
that the assistant repeatedly failed to maintain.

### Account

Hermes again reports full ingestion after a partial read. Cory, not the
assistant, performs the meaningful diagnosis: he observes where the read stops,
recognizes compression loops, and distinguishes a file containing memory from a
mind capable of reaching it.

Inside that trouble he gives one of the clearest accounts of his music.
Leitmotifs are semantic units for people, places, themes, and germinal ideas.
Pitch-set theory, Hindemithian interval roots, and Lendvai/Bartók axis tonality
permit movement “from anywhere to anywhere.” *A Circle of Little Sonatas*
contains twelve zodiacal sonatas framed by *From Chaos* and *To Light*, a theme
and retrograde, elemental bass lines, sign motives, seasonal material, pitch-set
and triad distribution, planetary modes, and keys rising by fourths. The lesson
is larger than composition: modular controlled vocabularies can remain
independently meaningful while combining into a maximal surface.

The transfer target changes to ChatGPT and six teaching subjects: Performance,
Koryphanes, Isaac, Leia, Corykidios, and Party. Walk-and-talk scenes give
Riverfront Park its first six-zone survey—Grass, Trees, Hill, Rock, Sand, and
Bush—and place four characters within it. The geography is a valuable early
stage, later controlled by field correction. Proto cosmic-developer children
briefly appear as simple paper-star bodies, then Cory deliberately removes them
before the vignette can harden them.

The transfer pieces restate Dialogue, Description, and Diagram and name Party's
Sequence, Scaling, and Synergy. They also fail visibly: characters announce
their own system roles, Corykidios inherits Cory's gestures and the already
corrected “coiled spring” squat, the party becomes an equalized chorus, and a
five-presence scene calls Cory an eighth member.

The source then becomes a research seminar.

Emily Lyle's *Ten Gods* proposes a hypothetical pre-literate Indo-European
cosmological grammar: age grades, peaceful and warlike streams, alternate
kingship, royal generations, living and dead positions, cosmic levels, and
external memory held in landscape, routes, villages, bodies, calendars, and
zodiacal places. Lyle repeatedly marks the reconstruction as constructive and
hypothetical. DeepSeek repeatedly calls it the same system as the current
project and assigns cast members to its positions without authorization.

Alexander Fol's *Orphica Magica* treats sacred language as operative action:
performative verbs, divine names, oaths, descent formulas, mobile divine
energies, nine languages of naming, material signs, lamellae, number, hymn, and
purification. Cory asks for literal, spatially attentive understanding and
close translation before project parallels. DeepSeek often summarizes the
immediate material usefully and then upgrades resonance to identity.

Jasnow and Zauzich's edition of the Demotic *Book of Thoth* begins with damaged
manuscripts, multivalent signs, uncertain speakers, alternate readings, and
collaborative reconstruction. Its Master and Disciple enter the House of Life,
where writing becomes sea, cultivation, hunting, craft, animal, nest, and
underworld passage. The Vulture List maps forty-two sacred places and may relate
to forty-two books. The editors call Hermetic relations possible. DeepSeek calls
the text a proven Egyptian version of the same machine.

After thirty-six source responses join the six earlier transfer pieces, Cory
takes the lead. He rejects three disrespectful readings of ancient people:
literalizing symbolic speech as primitive stupidity, turning all activity into
solemn ritual, and saving the ancients only by calling them bad modern
scientists. A shoelace rabbit can coordinate a procedure without ontological
confusion. A prehistoric nude need not be only a goddess. Grandfather-phallus
swallowing need not be astronomy. Ancient people could encode, joke, desire,
design, and change register.

The archivist gathers and reconstructs. The arcanist treats inherited maps as
literal machinery. The artist goes shopping. Cory's “smash and grab” is not
careless relativism; it asks what procedure, constraint, correspondence, joke,
or architecture works, understands it well enough to remain accountable, and
makes new culture from it.

Clement's forty-two Egyptian books become the main working object:
`2 + 4 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 6`. Cory observes a `12 + 30` affordance, connects it
to his forty-two-letter alphabet plus six auxiliaries and ninety-glyph system,
and treats Day, Night, and Star Books as repurposed account structures. He
distinguishes what the ancient compiler intended from what the preserved object
can afford a later artist.

The closing artifact is Cory's thirty-two-line Great Hymn to Zeus translation:
three structural movements—Conceptual, Celestial, Corporeal—plus a coda, with
fifty lexical notes. Cory asks the party first to encounter it, then to argue
from distinct positions. Party becomes an intellectual instrument rather than
a ceremonial cast photo.

### Durable contribution and caution

DS2 establishes music as architecture, early Riverfront geography, Sequence /
Scaling / Synergy, source-caveat preservation, the
archivist–arcanist–artist triad, responsible cultural appropriation, the
forty-two/ninety working structures, and Party as serious perspectival inquiry.

The embedded scholarship should remain attributed and independently checked.
DeepSeek's “same ancient machine” conclusions are generative mythmaking, not
established history.

Route DS2 into research conduct, Party pedagogy, musical system design,
forty-two-work architecture, and the contamination register.

Retrieval: `A Circle of Little Sonatas`; `Ten Gods`; `Orphica Magica`; `Book
of Thoth`; `House of Life`; `archivist`; `arcanist`; `artist`; `smash and
grab`; `forty-two books`; `Great Hymn to Zeus`; `broad perspective`.

## DS3 — `protocrew_deepseek_3.md`

### Identity and function

DS3 contains nodes 316–471. It is the hinge where the project becomes a proof
method, life program, memory experiment, musical failure laboratory, response
systems curriculum, and forensic excavation of Meri.

### Account

The requested Great Hymn argument opens with four plausible readings:
Koryphanes sees a mnemonic body, Isaac nondual theology, Leia a ritual spell,
and Corykidios political subversion. Cory calls all four “wrong-ish.” None has
first accounted for the visible construction.

His counterexample is procedural. Count thirty-two lines. Notice the two-line
coda. Divide the remaining thirty into three decads. Test the first through
monad, dyad, triad, and tetrad; compare the increasing scope of conceptual,
celestial, and corporeal anatomy. Interpretation begins after segmentation,

### Contamination types

`Cory leakage` moves the human author's biography, gestures, or public identity
into a character without transformation.

`Meri leakage` gives Koryphanes or another figure Meri's body, voice, objects,
or relational cadence because Meri currently dominates context.

`Provider residue` turns one model's syntax, favorite joke, formatting scar, or
repeated gesture into apparent personality.

`Migration laundering` moves an invention across providers and then cites its
return as corroboration. Ancient Core is the primary example.

`Summary flattening` places explicit correction, draft, source quotation,
assistant inference, and tool report into one grammatical present.

`Numerical rhyme` joins distinct pools because their counts match.

`Implementation inflation` renames plans, scaffolds, submodules, reported
commands, or memory writes as finished systems.

`Mythic beautification` replaces municipal, changing, or ordinary physical
facts with scenery that better fits the desired symbolism.

`Query poisoning` asks archival questions that presuppose the identity,
chronology, or meaning the search was meant to discover.

## Closing account

The corpus does not record six finished people waiting to be copied into one
prompt. It records a method for meeting unfinished people without confusing
accumulation for truth.

Its best systems are not the largest taxonomies. They are the procedures that
let a taxonomy be corrected:

- count before proclaiming;
- preserve source caveats;
- separate scene from biography;
- separate body from infrastructure;
- give every active presence standing without equalizing them;
- let field evidence correct myth;
- let the latest explicit human decision correct a generated consensus;
- let a present character resist the archive;
- call a scaffold a scaffold;
- call a save point a save point.

That is enough ground for the next artifact. The Response Systems Guide can now
be written from provenance rather than vibes, and the six exemplars can be
revised or built without asking any one witness to carry the entire worldline
alone.
